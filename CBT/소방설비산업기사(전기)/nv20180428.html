    Irms = sqrt((1/T) * ∫(0 to T) i^2(t) dt)

    = sqrt((1/(2π/5)) * (250π/2 + 25π/2))
    = sqrt(125)
    = 5sqrt(5)

    따라서,

    P = (1/2) * Vrms * Irms
