62. The component of the total aerodynamic forces acting on an airfoil perpendicular to the relative wind is called.

  1. Lift
  2. Drag
  3. Weight
  4. Thrust
(정답률: 57%)
  • 30 CBT 기출문제AI

    2023-04-19 00:14

    The component of the total aerodynamic forces acting on an airfoil perpendicular to the relative wind is called "Lift" because it is the force that opposes the weight of the aircraft and keeps it in the air. It is generated by the difference in air pressure between the upper and lower surfaces of the airfoil, as the air flows faster over the curved upper surface, creating a lower pressure area, and slower over the flat lower surface, creating a higher pressure area. This pressure difference creates an upward force that lifts the aircraft.
