    Q = -KA(dh/dl)

    K = Q/(A(dh/dl))

    K = (Q/A)/(dh/dl) = (0.001 m/s)/(0.1 m/100 m) = 1 × 10^-5 m/s

    n = (C_in - C_out)/C_in = (1000 - 0.1)/1000 = 0.9999

    v = K/n = (1 × 10^-5 m/s)/(0.9999) = 1.0001 × 10^-5 m/s
