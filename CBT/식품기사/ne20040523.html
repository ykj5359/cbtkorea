  1. acetyl CoA → L-mevalonic acid → squalene → lanosterol→ cholesterol
  2. acetyl CoA → lanosterol → squalene → L-mevalonic acid → cholesterol
  3. acetyl CoA → squalene → lanosterol → L-mevalonic acid → cholesterol
