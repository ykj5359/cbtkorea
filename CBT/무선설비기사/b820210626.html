  2. 65[Ω]
  3. 75[Ω]
  4. 80[Ω]

    $Z_{in} = Z_0 frac{Z_L+jZ_0tan(beta l)}{Z_0+jZ_Ltan(beta l)}$

    $Z_{short} = Z_0 frac{Z_L+jZ_0tan(beta l)}{Z_0+jZ_Ltan(beta l)}$

    $Z_0 = sqrt{Z_{in}Z_{short}}$

    $Z_0 = sqrt{30times187.5} = 75[Ω]$

    따라서 정답은 "75[Ω]"이다.
