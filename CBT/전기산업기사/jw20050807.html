    $$text{불평형률[%]}=frac{100}{sqrt{3}V_{text{line}}^2}left|sum_{i=1}^3V_iI_i^*right|$$

    $$V_{text{line}}=frac{1}{sqrt{3}}left|V_a-V_b+V_cright|=frac{1}{sqrt{3}}left|120-(-60-j80)+(-60+j80)right|=120text{V}$$
